# Gene Switch — Epigenetics of Exercise

**Exercise doesn't just burn calories. It flips switches in your DNA.**

🔗 **Try it live:** https://thanuhith.github.io/Gene-switch-simulator/

## What this is

Your genetic code doesn't change when you train — but *which genes get read* does. This is an interactive visualization of five real, well-studied exercise-responsive genes, showing how they shift from baseline to active expression when you switch from sedentary to active.

Toggle the switch and watch the DNA strand light up gene by gene.

## The genes

| Gene | What it does |
|---|---|
| **PGC-1α** (PPARGC1A) | Master regulator of mitochondrial growth. Endurance training reduces methylation at its promoter, raising expression within hours. |
| **VEGF** | Signals new capillaries to form around working muscle, improving oxygen delivery over weeks of training. |
| **BDNF** | Supports neuron growth and memory. Aerobic exercise is linked to lower promoter methylation and higher expression. |
| **IL-6** | Released by contracting muscle as a signaling molecule, triggering downstream metabolic and anti-inflammatory genes. |
| **SIRT1** | A cellular energy sensor activated by exercise-induced energy stress, helping switch on fat-burning genes. |

## Tech

Single self-contained HTML file — no dependencies, no build step, no backend. Just open `index.html` in a browser, or fork this repo and deploy it free on GitHub Pages.
